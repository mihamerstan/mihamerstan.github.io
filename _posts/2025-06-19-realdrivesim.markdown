This paper introduces a realistic multi-modal synthetic dataset for autonomous driving that supports both 2D computer vision and LiDAR-based perception tasks with annotations across up to 64 classes. We demonstrate the dataset's effectiveness through comprehensive evaluation across multiple applications and domains, reporting performance improvements compared to existing synthetic benchmarks.
